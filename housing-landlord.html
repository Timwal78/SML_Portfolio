Before Florida

  • Mortgage/HOA allows rental + voucher
  • Landlord insurance (not just HO-3)
  • Section-8-friendly local PM
  • Smoke/CO, locks, heat, electric, stairs
  • Lead disclosure if pre-1978
  • W-9, deposit info, ownership proof
  • Price at/under KHA 4BR standard + comps

HQS hotspots

  • Smoke + CO detectors
  • Heat / no active leaks
  • Secure exterior locks
  • Electrical covers / hazards
  • Handrails / trip hazards
  • Peeling paint (pre-1978)
  • Infestation / sewage / garbage
