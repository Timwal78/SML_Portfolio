# Executive Product Brief: TrustVerify AI
**Enterprise AI Vendor Risk Management & Autonomous Trust Scoring Platform**

## Executive Summary
**TrustVerify AI** is an enterprise AI vendor risk management platform designed for **Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs)**, **Chief Procurement Officers (CPOs)**, and Risk Executives. It provides continuous, automated, FICO-style TrustScores (300–850) across 5 critical compliance dimensions to detect shadow AI, mitigate vendor security breaches, and enforce contractual SLAs.
